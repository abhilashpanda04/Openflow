Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Does my voice audio leave my computer? expand_more
Never. By default, Openflow runs 100% locally on your Mac using WhisperKit CoreML and the Apple Silicon Neural Engine. Temporary audio buffers recorded during dictation are immediately deleted from disk as soon as transcription finishes or cancels.
Which Mac models are supported? expand_more
Openflow is built natively for Apple Silicon Macs (M1, M2, M3, M4 and their Pro/Max/Ultra variants) running macOS 14.0 Sonoma or macOS 15+ Sequoia. CoreML WhisperKit takes full advantage of Apple's unified memory and Neural Engine.
Can I customize the global shortcut? expand_more
Yes! In Openflow Settings > General, you can select convenient single-key presets such as Right Option, Fn / Globe, F5, or traditional multi-key shortcuts like Cmd + Shift + Space. Both push-to-talk (hold to speak) and tap-to-toggle are supported.
What voice commands are supported? expand_more
Openflow includes an intelligent VoiceCommandEngine. You can say “scratch that” to cancel dictation, “urgent all caps that” for uppercase, “select all”, “copy”, “paste”, “delete line”, “new line”, and “new paragraph”.
Is Openflow really free and open-source? expand_more
Yes. Openflow is licensed under the permissive Apache 2.0 license. There are no subscriptions, no locked tiers, no ads, and no tracking. You can inspect the complete source code, build it locally with Xcode/Swift, or contribute on GitHub.
