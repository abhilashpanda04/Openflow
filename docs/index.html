Core Capabilities

Hardware-accelerated voice to cursor.

Engineered exclusively for Apple Silicon. Explore the six architectural pillars that make Openflow instantaneous, private, and seamlessly integrated into every macOS application.

How it works

Openflow queries macOS's Accessibility API (AXUIElement) to discover the focused text input across any app—VS Code, Slack, Obsidian, Chrome, Terminal, or Notes—and streams tokens directly without polluting your system clipboard.

Activation Triggers
Double-tap fn or Globe key Global hotkey Option + Space 100% of native & Electron apps
Engine: WhisperKit CoreML
Target: Active AXUIElement
Dispatch Latency: <22ms
Clipboard Pollute: None
How it works

Instead of burning CPU cores or spinning laptop fans, Openflow executes Whisper encoder and decoder graphs directly on Apple Silicon's 16-core Neural Engine (ANE) with negligible battery consumption.

Hardware Optimization

Architectural Choice

Hardware-native vs Remote cloud APIs.

Why send your private voice recordings across the internet when your Mac already has a 16-core Neural Engine designed for machine learning inference?

OPENFLOW · LOCAL NEURAL

On-Device Apple Silicon

  • ✓ <24ms instant latency: Zero network roundtrips. Tokens stream as words leave your lips.
  • ✓ 100% Air-Gapped Privacy: No audio recordings ever leave your Mac. Works in airplane mode.
  • ✓ Free & Open Source: Apache 2.0 / MIT. No $18–$30/mo subscriptions or tier quotas.
  • ✓ Zero Idle Battery Drain: CoreML sleeps instantly when speech pauses.
CLOUD DICTATION APPS

Remote Cloud APIs

  • ✗ 600–1,200ms lag: Audio chunks must upload, transcribe remotely, and download.
  • ✗ Server-Side Voice Storage: Audio recordings saved on third-party cloud database clusters.
  • ✗ $15–$30/mo subscription costs: Locks everyday productivity behind recurring paywalls.
  • ✗ Fails without Wi-Fi: Completely non-functional on flights, subways, or remote locations.
